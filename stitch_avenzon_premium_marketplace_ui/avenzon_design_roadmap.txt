# AVENZON Public Marketplace Design Plan

## Phase 1: Core Commerce Flow
1. **Categories Page**: Modern grid with glassmorphic category cards and featured collections.
2. **Product Listing (PLP)**: Advanced sidebar filters, premium product grid with quick actions.
3. **Product Details (PDP)**: High-fidelity gallery, sticky purchase section, and comprehensive specifications.
4. **Search Results**: AI-powered results UI with sorting and advanced filtering.

## Phase 2: Checkout & Fulfillment
5. **Shopping Cart**: Premium drawer and full-page cart experience.
6. **Checkout**: Streamlined multi-step process for address, delivery, and payment.
7. **Order Tracking**: Visual timeline and courier status cards.

## Phase 3: Brand & Content
8. **Brand Store**: Branded landing pages for top sellers.
9. **Flash Sale / Deals**: Time-sensitive promotion layouts with countdowns.
10. **Company Pages**: About Us, Contact, Careers, and FAQ with premium storytelling.

## Design Principles
- **Whitespace**: Generous breathing room (24px-64px scales).
- **Interactivity**: Smooth hover states, micro-interactions for buttons.
- **Hierarchy**: Clear typography scaling using the INTER font family.
- **Trust**: Minimalist badges and clean data presentation.
